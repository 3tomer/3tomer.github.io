13 Words, 3 Games

Play memory, build sentences, then record your own. Tap a card below to hear a word.

Memory game

Turn over two cards. Find the English word and its Hebrew meaning.

Build the sentence

Tap the words in the right order. Tap a word in the line to send it back.

My voice

Choose a word. Listen to the example, then say your own sentence and record it. You can write it first if you like. Try to record at least 6!

My written sentences

Your sentences will appear here.
