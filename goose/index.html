The Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs

A comic for practising 10 words from the story. Read it, fill it, say it, then make it your own.

1 · ReadTap the yellow words 2 · FillComplete the bubbles 3 · Say itRecord sentences 4 · My comicWrite your own
This app can't play the sound. To listen, open the page in Chrome or Safari: in WhatsApp, download the file, then choose "Open with Chrome". Everything else works here too.
Part 1. Read the comic. Tap any yellow word to see what it means in Hebrew and to hear it.
Part 2. The words fell out of the comic! Tap a word, then tap the empty space where it belongs. Tap a filled space to take the word back.
Part 3. These are new sentences with the story words. For each one: press Listen, then read it out loud and record yourself. Try to get a green card for all 10!

Part 4. Now you are the author. Read the question, think, and write your own sentence with the word. When your sentence is ready, it becomes a comic panel. Then say it out loud. Try at least 5 words.
