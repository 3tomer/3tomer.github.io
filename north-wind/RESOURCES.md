# The North Wind and the Sun Resources

## Knowledge

- [Library of Congress: *The Aesop for Children* (1919, pictures by Milo Winter)](https://www.read.gov/aesop/index.htm)
  Free, public-domain, illustrated edition of the fables. Use for: the original wording of the fable and as the "read more" primary source.
- [Project Gutenberg: *The Aesop for Children*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/19994)
  Same 1919 book as plain text. Use for: checking the original text.
- [Wikipedia: The North Wind and the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_North_Wind_and_the_Sun)
  Background on the fable (Perry Index 46), its versions and its moral. Use for: teacher background only.
- [NWEA: Reading interventions after grade 4 should focus on both fluency and comprehension](https://www.nwea.org/blog/2020/how-to-reach-older-struggling-readers/)
  Supports repeated reading of the same text. Use for: the "read it three times" routine.
- [Edutopia: Improving oral reading fluency in middle school](https://www.edutopia.org/article/improving-oral-reading-fluency-middle-school)
  Recorded readings, modelling and short comprehension questions during fluency practice. Use for: design of later lessons (recording the student's reading).
- [Edutopia: Supporting struggling readers with grade-level texts](https://www.edutopia.org/article/supporting-struggling-readers-navigating-grade-level-texts/)
  Scaffolds from most to least support (modelling, reading together, reading alone). Use for: ordering the steps in each lesson.
