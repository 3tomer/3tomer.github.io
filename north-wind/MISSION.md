# Mission: The North Wind and the Sun

## Why
A 6th grader who finds reading hard needs to read a whole English story by themselves, understand it, and feel successful. The story is Aesop's *The North Wind and the Sun*, told in short sentences with pictures. (Assumed from the request. Check with the teacher.)

## Success looks like
- Reads the 6-panel story out loud without help after listening to it twice
- Knows the 9 story words (argue, stronger, traveller, cloak, blow, tighter, shine, gentle, rough) in English and Hebrew
- Answers the 4 comprehension questions correctly without looking back
- Says the lesson of the fable in their own words

## Constraints
- English is a foreign language; Hebrew is the home language (same as the other lessons on this site)
- Reading is slow and effortful, so each lesson must be short, with read-aloud and big, clear text
- Works on a phone or school computer in the browser

## Out of scope
- Grammar teaching (past tense is only noted next to the word)
- The longer 1919 version of the fable, except as an extra with a grown-up
