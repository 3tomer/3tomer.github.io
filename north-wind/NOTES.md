# Notes

- Audience: a 6th grader who struggles with reading. The person setting this up is their teacher or parent.
- Site style: matches the other lessons in this repo (e.g. `goose/`): tappable yellow words with Hebrew, read-aloud, picture panels.
- Lesson 1 = the full story + 4-question check. Spaced practice: ask the student to redo the quiz the next day without rereading.
- Voice: every spoken text is a recorded MP3 in `audio/` (Kokoro voice model, af_heart, speed 0.85), listed in `audio/audio.js`. The browser voice is only a fallback. New lessons need new recordings.
- Ideas for next lessons: put the pictures in order and retell; record yourself reading (as in `word-games/`); fill the missing word in the story.
- Waiting on the teacher: how the student did, and whether the Hebrew translations are right.
