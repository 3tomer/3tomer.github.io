A story by Aesop · Lesson 1

The North Wind and the Sun

A short story in 6 pictures. Tap a yellow word to see it in Hebrew and hear it.

This app can't play the voice. Open the page in Chrome or Safari to listen.

Read it three times

  1. Listen. Press Read me the story. Follow the words with your finger.
  2. Read with the voice. Press it again and read out loud at the same time.
  3. Read alone. Read it out loud by yourself. Tap any word you forget.

Check yourself

What happened?

Try to answer without looking back at the story. If you are wrong, you can try again.
